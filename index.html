ℹ️ Scenario Estimation Notice & Assumptions

The What-If Simulator applies empirical sensitivity weights derived from our feature attribution analysis to estimate directional risk impact under hypothetical contract, pricing, and support interventions. This serves as a real-time decision-support tool and is mathematically distinct from full ML model re-inference.
